Overview

What is Recoup?

Recoup is an AI revenue-recovery agent for failed payments. It replaces crude, indiscriminate retry scripts with a verifiable, multi-stage recovery architecture designed specifically for digital commerce.

Capabilities

What Recoup does

Five bounded responsibilities operating in sequence to safely maximize payment recovery rate without customer friction or financial risk.

01 DETECT Detect Identify failed payment attempts and compute live revenue at risk across active merchant batches.
02 DIAGNOSE Diagnose Determine the likely failure category using deterministic rules and AI for ambiguous cases.
03 GUARD Guard Apply hard financial and risk boundaries before any action can execute.
04 EXECUTE Execute Dispatch only permitted recovery actions through the Razorpay Test Mode execution layer.
05 VERIFY Verify Independently confirm whether the payment actually succeeded before counting revenue as recovered.
Context & Need

Why it exists

Lost Revenue 01
Failed payments leave otherwise recoverable revenue behind, hurting merchant LTV and unit economics.
Blind Retries 02
Retrying everything immediately is unsafe, wastes bank API limits, and triggers unnecessary customer friction.
Risk 03
Fraud-risk and high-value transactions require controlled escalation rather than automated retries.
Verified Recovery 04
Revenue is counted as recovered only after independent verification from payment capture APIs.

Safety & Governance

AI recommends. Code decides.

Strict Boundary Architecture

The AI layer is advisory only. It can diagnose ambiguous failures and propose a recovery action, but it cannot authorize transactions, modify guardrails, write recovery outcomes, or directly execute Razorpay actions.

Deterministic policy logic remains the authorization boundary.
